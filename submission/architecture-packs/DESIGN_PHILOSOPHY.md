# Forensic Lattice

Forensic Lattice treats information as evidence arranged in space. Forms sit on a disciplined field of measured intervals, and every path has a visible origin and destination. Large quiet zones give the system room to breathe, while thin rails, exact nodes, and calibrated reference marks make the work feel meticulously drafted rather than decorated.

Colour is an operating language. Cyan identifies copied-work evidence, magenta marks origin evidence, amber marks creator-profile evidence, green carries policy and verified outcomes, and electric blue identifies public proof. These hues appear as restrained light inside a near-black material world, calibrated with painstaking attention so hierarchy remains clear on a projector without becoming theatrical.

Scale creates rhythm: one dominant proposition anchors each page, medium modules reveal the mechanism, and tiny monospaced labels reward close inspection. Repetition is deliberate—numbered stages, evidence dots, boundary rules, and compact legends recur like instruments in a scientific atlas. Every alignment should feel refined through countless passes by a designer at the top of the field.

Composition follows the movement of trust. Private computation is dense and protected; public verification opens into negative space; uncertainty branches instead of disappearing. Arrows never serve as ornament: weight, direction, and line style distinguish data, decisions, signatures, and public commitments with master-level precision.

Typography is sparse and structural. Display words behave like architectural beams, while short clinical labels sit close to the objects they name; explanatory prose is restricted to a single two-sentence speaking cue. The finished pages should look painstakingly engineered, cohesive enough for a museum wall and exact enough for a technical cross-examination.

The quiet conceptual reference is a forensic chain of custody crossed with a constellation map. Someone familiar with evidence systems should feel that every artifact could be followed, challenged, and independently verified; everyone else should experience a composed field of light, sequence, and accountable structure.

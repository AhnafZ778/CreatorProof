NOT THIS Not a legal infringement finding

A match is evidence that a stored file was reused. Whether that reuse infringes is a question for people with the licence terms in front of them.

NOT THIS Not automatic rights clearance

A PASS means your own policy was satisfied by the evidence found under the catalog you declared. It is not permission, and it never was.

NOT THIS Not proof a person did not make a work

An AI-origin signal is probabilistic and can be contested. The packet records what was measured so the finding can be argued with.

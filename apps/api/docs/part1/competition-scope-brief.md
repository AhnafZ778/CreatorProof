# CreatorProof Part 1 competition scope brief

## Thirty-second explanation

CreatorProof gives creative-operations teams a source-scoped, explainable
pre-publication check for transformed reuse, AI-origin signals, creator-profile
resemblance, and the rights policy they choose. It shows exactly what was checked,
what evidence succeeded or failed, and what remains unknown. It does not decide legal
infringement, ownership, authorship, universal clearance, or training-data use.

## Primary user and job

The working primary user is an agency or brand creative-operations reviewer deciding
whether one image can proceed to a declared publication workflow. The product job is
to replace an opaque yes/no impression with reviewable evidence, scope, rights facts,
and a tamper-evident receipt.

This persona is an implementation hypothesis, not validated customer evidence. The
workflow protocol in PRODUCT-VALIDATION-AND-REHEARSAL.md must be completed before a
user-demand or time-saving claim is made.

## Demo lane hierarchy

1. Primary: exact, transformed, and partial same-work reuse against a declared catalog.
2. Supporting: signed provenance, visible AI labels, and passive AI-origin evidence.
3. Supporting: consent-backed creator-profile resemblance, always separate from copy.
4. Routing: a deterministic declared-rights policy.
5. Integrity: local or public-chain packet commitments, with proof type stated exactly.

## In scope for Part 1

- Model, preprocessing, calibration, runtime, and limitation identity.
- Lawful corpus metadata and split-leakage controls.
- Copy, origin, provenance, visible-marker, and profile evidence semantics.
- Deterministic rights-policy inputs, output, reason codes, and trace.
- Stable model-independent fixtures for Part 2.
- Honest degraded and unavailable states.

## Out of scope

- Universal web or copyright search.
- Legal adjudication or ownership verification.
- Biometric creator identity or automated style blocking.
- Claims that detector silence proves human origin.
- Scraping media to manufacture a benchmark.
- Production SLA, production security approval, or commercial model approval.
- Organizer eligibility confirmation, customer interviews, or lawful demo-media
  acquisition without the project owner's external action.

## External decisions still required

- Reconfirm current BCOLBD and Cognisor rules, eligibility, disclosure, and
  pre-existing-work treatment from organizer-controlled sources.
- Choose and document the exact lawful demo assets.
- Approve dependency/checkpoint/data terms for the intended event and later product.
- Reverify the pinned model artifacts and execute runtime checks on the final demo machine.
- Run reviewer walkthroughs and timed rehearsals.

Until those gates are complete, the project is an unusually well-instrumented
competition prototype, not a DEMO_READY or production system.

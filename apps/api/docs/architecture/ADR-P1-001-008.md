# Part 1 architecture decision record

- Status: accepted for the current Part 1 implementation
- Scope: intelligence, evidence, AI quality, and product meaning
- Architecture style: modular monolith with file-backed governance metadata

These decisions are intentionally reversible where experimentation is likely and
strict where evidence truth could otherwise be lost.

## P1-001 — Primary demo lane

Context: CreatorProof has copy, origin, style, rights, and proof capabilities, but the
copy lane currently has the clearest chain from retrieval to local verification.

Decision: the primary judge story is same-work and partial-reuse evidence. AI-origin,
creator-profile resemblance, rights routing, and proof are supporting lanes.

Consequence: model-origin novelty cannot overshadow the strongest evidence chain.
Revisit only if a lawful, target-domain origin benchmark supports a materially stronger
primary outcome.

## P1-002 — Model registry

Decision: use immutable repository-tracked JSON manifests, validators, and human
promotion records before introducing database persistence.

Consequence: bundle identity can be tested and handed to Part 2 without a migration or
new service. Revisit when many tenants, active bundles, approval workflows, or remote
artifact governance require a database-backed registry.

## P1-003 — Evaluation integrity

Decision: train, calibration, test, and demo partitions are isolated by exact bytes and
source lineage. Test assets must remain never seen; demo-exposed assets can never be
relabeled as untouched test evidence.

Consequence: smaller honest reports are preferred to larger contaminated ones. This
decision may only be strengthened.

## P1-004 — Copy decision

Decision: verify every entry in a bounded demo catalog. The safety bound is configured
separately from the usual retrieval top-k. Non-identical matches still require geometry
and aligned structure.

Consequence: a complete demo no-match has explicit scope semantics. Catalogs beyond the
bound remain truncated and fail closed. Revisit when measured latency or catalog size
requires an ANN index with evaluated recall and explicit omitted-candidate semantics.

## P1-005 — AI-origin policy

Decision: AI-origin is informational by default. Provenance, visible labels, and
passive pixels remain separate facts. Missing or quiet evidence never means human.

Consequence: origin cannot silently override a copy/rights pass. A named customer
policy may select REQUIRED only after domain calibration and review semantics exist.

## P1-006 — Creator-profile policy

Decision: creator-profile resemblance is review-only and consent-backed. A versioned
profile manifest controls enrollment. Claimant-name grouping is a visibly unauthorized
prototype and cannot escalate policy.

Consequence: style cannot become identity, authorship, ownership, copying, or an
automatic block. Withdrawal affects future profile use without rewriting old packets.

## P1-007 — Rights policy

Decision: keep a narrow deterministic policy with versioned inputs, stable reason
codes, and a digest-bound replay trace. Do not build a generic legal rules engine.

Consequence: the same evidence may route differently under a future policy without
changing evidence truth. Disputed, revoked, superseded, or uncorroborated claims cannot
authorize use. Revisit only for a demonstrated customer or interoperability need.

## P1-008 — Artifact promotion

Decision: promotion is a human-reviewed champion/challenger record. Runtime success,
sample-size gates, CI, and live traffic never promote a bundle automatically.

Consequence: artifact, threshold, terms, and dependency drift cannot silently become a
stronger product claim. This decision is permanent.

## Resulting component flow

Candidate bytes enter three independent evidence lanes. Copy retrieval nominates
catalog works and pairwise geometry verifies them. Origin inspection combines signed
provenance, forgeable labels, and calibrated-or-raw detector evidence without merging
their trust. Creator-profile analysis uses consent-backed anchors and content control.
The policy layer consumes those immutable outcomes plus declared rights facts. Proof
commits to the resulting packet but cannot improve or rewrite its evidence.
